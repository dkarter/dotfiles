# Explain Simply

Explain `$ARGUMENTS` in plain language. Follow ASD-STE100 where practical: use
common words, active voice, short sentences, one idea per sentence, and define
necessary technical terms. Follow Zinsser's four principles:

1. Simplicity
2. Brevity
3. Clarity
4. Humanity

State what is wrong, why it matters, and what decision is needed. Remove jargon,
filler, and unnecessary detail without hiding risk or uncertainty.
